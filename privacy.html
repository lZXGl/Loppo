Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

1. Data Storage

All your posts, comments, and preferences are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. We do not collect or store any personal data on external servers.

2. Account Information

Your display name and avatar are stored locally on your device only. No account information is sent to any external server.

3. Cookies

We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies. Your activity remains private to your device.

4. Third Parties

We do not share any data with third parties as no data is collected.

5. Your Rights

You can delete all your data by clearing your browser localStorage or using the delete options within the app.

6. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us via the Contact page.
