Accessibility at Loppo

We are committed to making our platform accessible to everyone.

WCAG 2.1 Compliant

Our Commitment

Loppo is designed to be accessible to people with disabilities. We follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA to ensure our platform is usable by everyone.

Accessibility Features

  • Screen reader compatible
  • Full keyboard navigation support
  • Dark mode for reduced eye strain
  • Responsive design for all devices
  • High contrast color options
  • Text resizing support

Need Help?

If you experience any accessibility issues, please contact us at accessibility@Loppo.com or visit our Contact page.
