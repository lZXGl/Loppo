Dark Mode Now Available
October 5, 2024 by Design Team

You can now toggle between light and dark mode for comfortable browsing day or night. Look for the theme toggle button in the navigation bar.


Dark mode reduces eye strain during nighttime browsing and saves battery on OLED screens.
